Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

PropReach ("we", "our", "us") is a WhatsApp broadcast and CRM tool for businesses. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the service:

  • Contact data — Names, phone numbers, and group labels you add to PropReach for messaging campaigns.
  • Message logs — Records of messages sent and received through the WhatsApp Business API, including delivery status.
  • Account credentials — Your WhatsApp Business API credentials (stored securely and used solely to send messages on your behalf).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send WhatsApp template messages to your contacts via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • To display delivery reports, reply tracking, and campaign analytics in your dashboard.
  • To process opt-out requests from recipients automatically.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is transmitted to Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API) solely for message delivery purposes, in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored securely in Supabase with encryption at rest. API credentials are stored as encrypted values and are never exposed to the frontend.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You may delete contacts, messages, and campaign data at any time through the PropReach dashboard. Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

6. User Rights

You have the right to access, export, or delete your data at any time. Recipients of your messages may opt out by replying with "STOP", and they will be automatically blocked from future messages.
